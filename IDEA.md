Tieng-trung is a Mandarin Chinese learning toolkit designed specifically for Vietnamese speakers, leveraging shared Sino-Vietnamese (Hán-Việt) linguistic roots to accelerate vocabulary acquisition.

* Build an interactive text reader that annotates Chinese characters with both Pinyin and Hán-Việt readings side-by-side on hover.
* Generate spaced-repetition (SRS) flashcards automatically from imported texts, highlighting direct cognate connections between Vietnamese and Mandarin words.
* Provide a grammar comparison module that flags common structural pitfalls where Vietnamese and Mandarin syntax diverge.
* Include an audio pronunciation trainer focusing on Mandarin tones and retroflex consonants that lack direct Vietnamese equivalents.
